English

BOX1R ("the game") is a single-player boxing game that runs entirely on your device.

  • No personal data is collected. The game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information.
  • Works offline. The game does not need an internet connection and does not send data over the network.
  • No ads. The game contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking. The game uses no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No in-app purchases. The game offers nothing for sale inside the app.
  • Data on your device. Your settings, career progress, locker-room choices (including the ring name you type), saved replays and photo-mode pictures are saved only on your device. They never leave it, and deleting the game deletes them.
  • Children. Because no personal data is collected, the game collects none from children either.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
